Premium Manufacturing & Engineering Excellence

SREW Projects is a premier manufacturer specializing in the end-to-end design, manufacturing, and turnkey installation of high-capacity crushing, screening, and material handling plants. We serve top-tier operators across the mining, aggregate, and infrastructure sectors with a relentless commitment to build quality and operational uptime.

Our integrated ecosystem (featuring CNC machining, heavy-duty fabrication, and PLC automation) ensures that every plant meets rigorous global standards for capacity, efficiency, and real-world performance.

Leadership

Mr. Ranga Reddy J.

Founder & Head of Operations

With over 30 years of field-tested expertise, Ranga Reddy leads SREW Projects with a focus on reliability and execution quality. His extensive career includes serving as Operations Head for major projects in Kenya and key roles at Srisailam and Singareni Collieries.

Having managed industrial operations across India, Africa, and beyond, he bridges local conditions with international standards to deliver practical, robust engineering solutions. A recipient of the 'Best Engineer Award' by Balaji Industries, his hands-on leadership at SREW Projects ensures every project is built for durability and long-term performance.
