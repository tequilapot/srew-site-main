Overview

SREW Projects VSI Crusher is designed for high performance and consistent quality of manufactured sand. It is widely used in the final stage of crushing process to produce high quality cubical shaped sand which meets construction industry standards.

How It Works

Material is fed into the center of the rotor through the feed tube. The high speed rotor accelerates the material and throws it against the anvil ring. The material is then crushed by impact and attrition between rock on rock inside the crushing chamber.
