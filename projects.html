Project FAQs

Common questions about our project capabilities

How many projects completed?

SREW Projects has successfully delivered over 120 turnkey projects for 45+ clients across India in mining and recycling.

Turnkey project types?

We deliver crushing plants, M-Sand plants, aggregate production facilities, and C&D waste recycling plants.

Installation services?

Yes, we provide end-to-end services including site assessment, manufacturing, installation, and commissioning.

Project locations?

Our major projects are located in Odisha, West Bengal, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and across the Indian subcontinent.

After-sales support?

We provide comprehensive after-sales support, including operator training, maintenance, and genuine spare parts supply.

Client portfolio size?

We have a portfolio of 45+ repeat clients, including top-tier operators in the mining and infrastructure sectors.

Design to delivery time?

Standard turnkey projects are delivered within 8–12 weeks from design finalization to plant commissioning on site.

Automation in plants?

Our plants feature advanced PLC-based automation to ensure maximum yield, safety, and operational efficiency.

Consultation services?

We offer technical consultation for plant optimization and modernization of existing crushing and screening setups.
