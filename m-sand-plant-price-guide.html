Manufactured Sand (M-Sand) Plant Setup Cost & Price Guide

Comprehensive B2B guide to M-sand plant setup costs, vertical shaft impactor (VSI) selection, sand washing plants, and ROI payback analysis.

Published: May 2026 | SREW Projects Precision Engineers

Manufactured Sand (M-Sand) has emerged as the ultimate eco-friendly alternative to depleting river sand. With absolute regulatory crackdowns on riverbed dredging, the demand for high-quality concrete and plaster sand has skyrocketed. A modern M-Sand plant utilizes dry or wet processing technology to produce aggregate sand matching IS-383 Zone II particle distribution guidelines. SREW Projects' custom M-Sand setups, featuring proprietary VSI crushing technology, offer low CAPEX and quick ROI.

1. Essential Equipment Needed for M-Sand Production

To convert raw rock feed (usually 10-20mm grit) into NHAI-compliant plaster and concrete sand, a highly specific processing loop is required. SREW Projects' M-Sand systems include:

  • Primary Feed Hopper: Regulates the flow of 10-20mm aggregate feed.
  • SREW Projects VSI Crusher (Vertical Shaft Impactor): The core machine. It utilizes a rock-on-rock crushing action to shape particles perfectly.
  • Vibrating Screen: Separates concrete sand, plaster sand, and oversize recycle lines.
  • Wet Washing (Optional): SREW Projects Sand Washing Plant or Hydrocyclone Classifier (for removing excess micro-fines under 75 microns).

Frequently Asked Questions

Concrete sand (Zone II) is coarser, typically ranging from 150 microns to 4.75mm, which matches concrete design guidelines. Plaster sand (Zone IV) is much finer (under 2.36mm) and requires very low silt/dust contents (under 5%) to ensure high plaster finishing quality.

Dry processing utilizes air air-classifiers (dry dust collectors) to extract excess fines. Wet processing utilizes standard sand washing plants and hydrocyclones to wash away clay and micro-fines, which is highly recommended for muddy quarry rock feeds.
