Sector Demands & SREW Approach

The construction and infrastructure sector demands uncompromising gradation control and cubical aggregate shape index to meet stringent NHAI (National Highways Authority of India) and IRC road paving codes. SREW designs integrated high-capacity crushing circuits utilizing secondary Cone crushers and high-speed Vertical Shaft Impactors (VSI) to produce top-tier concrete aggregates (10mm, 20mm, 40mm) and high-density Manufactured Sand (M-Sand). Our mobile skid units provide infrastructure contractors with the power to process materials right on-site.
