Frequently asked questions

Which stainless steel grades do you fabricate?

We fabricate in the stainless steel grade you specify, most commonly SS 304 and SS 316, with the sheet thickness agreed in your quotation.

Can you make SS tanks to custom sizes?

Yes. SS tanks are made to order. Share the capacity, orientation (vertical or horizontal), nozzle positions and the liquid to be stored, and we will quote.

Do you supply commercial kitchen equipment outside Hyderabad?

Yes. We fabricate at our Hyderabad works and dispatch across India. Installation can be arranged on request.

How do I get a price for SS fabrication?

Call or WhatsApp +91 8790759460, or use the quotation form with your drawing or requirement. Prices depend on grade, thickness and size.
