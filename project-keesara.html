Keesara, Telangana | NHAI Infrastructure Operations

Engineering Approach & Build

Supplying graded aggregates for strict NHAI expressway pavement specs requires precise particle size distribution. SREW Projects designed and configured a 150 TPH circuit at Keesara, incorporating a primary Jaw crusher alongside an advanced single-cylinder hydraulic Cone Crusher. Supported by a triple-deck high-frequency vibrating screen and storage silos, SREW Projects ensured flawless material gradation and centralized automated operation.

Plant Engineering Material Flow Process

1
Granite Quarrying

Hard granite stone fed into primary loader hopper.

2
Primary Jaw

Heavy primary breaking down to under 140mm size.

3
Hydraulic Cone

Hydraulic S100 Cone crusher performs cubical shaping.

4
Triple-Deck Screen

Precision grading to meet IRC and NHAI paving codes.

Project Performance & Outcome

The Keesara plant successfully supplied over 4 lakh metric tonnes of certified aggregates for high-speed expressways across Telangana. The hydraulic automated cone gap setting ensured zero aggregate shape failures during strict government lab tests.
