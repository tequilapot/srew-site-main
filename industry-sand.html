Sector Demands & SREW Projects Approach

Due to strict bans on river sand dredging across major Indian states, Manufactured Sand (M-Sand) and plaster sand have become the absolute standard. SREW Projects delivers comprehensive sand processing turnkey plants, featuring heavy-duty VSI rock-on-rock crushers for sand shaping, spiral dewatering sand washers for fine clay removal, and high-efficiency thermal LPG/gas-fired Sand Dryers to achieve moisture levels below 1% for dry-mix packaging. SREW Projects structural silo systems ensure clean sand storage.

Custom-Sized Circuit Sizing Flowchart Map

1
VSI Sand Maker

Rock-on-rock high velocity impact shapes raw grit to sand.

2
Sand Washing

Fine spiral sand washers eliminate clay and mud residues.

3
Thermal Sand Dryer

LPG-fired dryer dries sand for dry-mix plaster packaging.

4
Storage Silos

Enclosed steel silos preserve dried sand from atmospheric moisture.
