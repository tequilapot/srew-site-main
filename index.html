Material-led crushing

A feeder supplies material to the selected crusher. Jaw, cone or impact stages are chosen for the material and required reduction.

Equipment and safety scope

Equipment guarding, isolation and overload protection are specified for the selected machines and control system. Confirm the safety scope before ordering.

Screening and conveying

Screens separate material into the required sizes. Conveyors transfer graded product and can return oversize material for further crushing.

Site and relocation planning

Transport dimensions, lifting, ground preparation, utilities and commissioning arrangements are confirmed in the project-specific layout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about SREW Projects and our solutions

What is SREW Projects' core expertise?

SREW Projects specializes in design, manufacturing, and turnkey installation of high-capacity crushing, screening, and material handling plants.

What are your plant capacities?

We configure modular plants around feed material, target output, site conditions and project requirements for mining, aggregates and C&D recycling.

Where is SREW Projects located?

Our manufacturing facility and headquarters are located in Dullapally, Hyderabad, Telangana, serving clients globally.

What industries do you serve?

We serve Mining, Construction, Infrastructure, and C&D Waste Recycling sectors with precision engineering solutions.

Do you provide turnkey solutions?

Yes, we offer complete turnkey services including plant design, manufacturing, installation, commissioning, and support.

Is SREW Projects an ISO certified company?

Yes, SREW Projects is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, ensuring international quality and manufacturing standards.

What equipment do you manufacture?

Do you handle recycling projects?
