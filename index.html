S R ENGG WORKS (SREW) is a premier manufacturer dedicated to heavy-duty industrial fabrication and precision engineering. We specialize in the end-to-end design, proprietary manufacturing, and turnkey installation of high-capacity crushing, screening, sand processing, and material handling plants.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about S R ENGG WORKS and our solutions

What is SREW's core expertise?

SREW specializes in design, manufacturing, and turnkey installation of high-capacity crushing, screening, and material handling plants.
