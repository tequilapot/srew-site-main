Mobile Stone Crusher Plants – Standard Range 20–250 TPH

SREW Projects supplies wheel-mounted and modular crushing and screening configurations for quarry and project sites. The standard plant category covers 20–250 TPH; the models listed below start at 50 TPH. Plant selection, foundations, transport and commissioning requirements are confirmed for each site.

For smaller outputs, see mini mobile plants, 2–10 TPH.

Frequently asked questions

What is the price of a mobile stone crusher plant in India?

Published standard mobile models: mobile cone plant ₹38,50,000; 50 TPH jaw plant ₹56,00,000; 200 TPH ₹1,20,00,000; 250 TPH ₹1,30,00,000. Prices are indicative, + GST, ex-works Hyderabad. Mini mobile prices are listed separately.

What capacities do you build?

Standard category: 20–250 TPH. Listed standard mobile models begin at 50 TPH. Mini mobile plants cover 2–10 TPH. Availability and the final layout require a project quotation.

Wheel-mounted or modular?

Wheel-mounted plants suit frequent relocation; modular skid plants suit semi-permanent sites with faster installation than a fixed plant.
