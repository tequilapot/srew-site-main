Overview

SREW Projects Cone Crushers represent the pinnacle of secondary and tertiary crushing technology. Combining high speed, large stroke, and optimized crushing cavities, they deliver unparalleled performance and excellent product cubicity for premium aggregates.

Smart Control

PLC automation

Multiple Cavities

Hydraulic protection

Excellent Shape

Laminated crushing

How It Works

The mantle performs a gyratory motion within the concave bowl, driven by an eccentric sleeve. As material flows down the crushing chamber, it is repeatedly compressed and crushed against the manganese liners. The laminated crushing action ensures excellent cubicity.
