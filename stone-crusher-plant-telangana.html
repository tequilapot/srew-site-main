Telangana projects

Keesara: 150 TPH plant for NHAI expressway aggregates

At Keesara we designed a 150 TPH circuit to supply graded aggregate to NHAI pavement specifications. Hard granite is fed to a primary jaw crusher that reduces it below 140 mm. An SREW Projects S100 single-cylinder hydraulic cone crusher then shapes the stone, and a 3-deck high-frequency vibrating screen grades it to IRC and NHAI codes before it goes to storage silos. The plant runs with centralized automated operation and has supplied over 4 lakh metric tonnes of certified aggregates for expressways across Telangana.

Singareni Collieries: 250 TPH plant, delivered 2020

For Singareni Collieries we delivered a 250 TPH plant for continuous 24/7 operation. An apron feeder supplies dual heavy-duty toggle jaw crushers, a grizzly bypass sends pre-crushed material straight to the secondary circuit, and an SREW Projects HSI impact crusher does the secondary reduction. Dual 4-deck heavy vibrating screens size the product, and a central PLC panel controls the plant. Manganese liners keep maintenance downtime low, and the bypass channel and dual screens kept output steady through monsoon operations.

Hyderabad: C&D waste recycling

In Hyderabad we also built a 40–50 TPH C&D waste recycling plant with a trommel, magnetic separator, C&D-modified jaw crusher and LPG-fired sand dryer.
